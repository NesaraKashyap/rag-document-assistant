# Product FAQ

## What is NimbusDesk?
NimbusDesk is a customer support ticketing platform that helps teams manage inbound support requests across email, chat, and social channels from a single inbox.

## What plans are available?
- **Starter**: $15/user/month. Includes email support channel, up to 3 team members, and 500 tickets/month.
- **Pro**: $45/user/month. Includes email, chat, and social channels, unlimited team members, and 5,000 tickets/month.
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing. Includes all Pro features plus SSO, HIPAA add-on, dedicated account manager, and unlimited tickets.

## Can I switch plans mid-cycle?
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are billed pro-rated for the remainder of the cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

## Does NimbusDesk integrate with Slack?
Yes, Slack integration is available on all plans. Ticket notifications, assignment alerts, and SLA breach warnings can be routed to a specified Slack channel.

## What happens if I exceed my ticket quota?
On the Starter plan, exceeding 500 tickets/month triggers a $0.10 per additional ticket overage charge. Pro and Enterprise plans do not have hard overage charges but may receive a notification recommending a plan upgrade if usage is consistently high.

## Is there an API?
Yes. The REST API is available on Pro and Enterprise plans. Starter plan customers can purchase API access as an add-on for $20/month, which includes 10,000 API calls; additional calls are billed at $0.001 each.

## How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; see the Refund Policy document for details on refund eligibility.
