# Refund Policy

## Standard Refunds
Customers on the Starter and Pro plans may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. Refund requests must be submitted through the billing portal or by emailing billing@nimbusdesk.io.

## Enterprise Refunds
Enterprise customers (annual contracts over $10,000) are subject to a different policy. Refunds are only issued on a pro-rated basis if the contract is cancelled within the first 30 days. After 30 days, no refunds are issued for the remaining contract term, though customers may downgrade at their next renewal date.

## Non-Refundable Items
- Custom onboarding or implementation services
- Add-on API credit packs once consumed
- Any usage beyond the plan's included quota

## Processing Time
Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days and returned to the original payment method. Enterprise refunds processed via invoice/wire transfer may take up to 15 business days.

## Special Circumstances
If a customer experiences a verified service outage lasting more than 4 consecutive hours, they are entitled to a service credit equal to 10% of their monthly subscription fee, applied automatically to the next billing cycle. This is separate from the standard refund policy and does not require a support ticket.
