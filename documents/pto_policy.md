# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue PTO at a rate of 1.5 days per month, capping at 18 days per calendar year. PTO begins accruing from the first day of employment, but new hires cannot use accrued PTO during their first 90 days.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Any unused days beyond that are forfeited on December 31st, except where state law requires otherwise (e.g., California employees are not subject to forfeiture).

## Requesting Time Off
PTO requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for requests under 3 days, and at least 3 weeks in advance for requests of 3 or more consecutive days. Manager approval is required.

## Sick Leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO and is not capped. Employees may use sick leave for personal illness, medical appointments, or to care for an immediate family member. No advance notice is required for sick leave, but employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

## Holidays
NimbusDesk observes 10 paid company holidays per year, listed in the shared company calendar. Employees in regions with different public holidays may swap up to 2 company holidays for locally observed ones, with manager approval.

## Parental Leave
Employees are eligible for 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or fostering of a child, provided they have been employed for at least 6 months prior to the leave start date. Parental leave does not draw from standard PTO balance.

## Unused PTO at Termination
Upon voluntary or involuntary termination, unused accrued PTO is paid out at the employee's current base salary rate, in accordance with state law. This does not apply to sick leave, which is not paid out upon termination.
